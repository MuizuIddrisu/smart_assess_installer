What is Smart Assess?

Smart Assess is a desktop school management system designed specifically for Ghanaian basic schools — from KG to JHS. It runs directly on your school's Windows computer with or without internet. On paid plans, when you reconnect it syncs your data to the cloud, so a broken or stolen computer doesn't mean lost records.

Unlike web-based systems that break the moment your internet goes down, Smart Assess keeps working. Your teachers enter scores, attendance, and fees in the classroom — and everything is ready for report card printing day without a single data entry scramble.

Everything Your School Needs, In One App

No subscriptions to five different tools. No training your staff on complicated software. Everything is in one place, built the way Ghanaian schools actually work.

Student Biodata

Score Entry & Grading

Enter class scores, exams, and mock results. The system auto-calculates averages, positions, and grades based on your school's own grading scale.

Report Cards

Generate beautiful, professional A4 and A5 report cards for JHS and primary classes. Print individually or the entire class in one click.

Attendance Tracking

Record days present and absent per student per term. Attendance feeds directly into report cards automatically.

Fee Management

Set fees per level, record payments and print receipts. See defaulters by class or by fee, track expenses and IGF, and get a term report with charts.

Parent SMS Alerts

Send results with every subject's score, fee receipts and reminders listing each fee, or custom notices — to one parent, a class or the whole school, on any network.
